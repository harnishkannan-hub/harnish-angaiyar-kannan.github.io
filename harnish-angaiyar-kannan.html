Biography

About Me

Harnish Angaiyar Kannan is a Master of Biotechnology (MBIOT) candidate at Texas A&M University (GPA 4.0/4.0) with dual active graduate research appointments in preclinical neuroscience and tumor immunology.

He conducts independent research in Dr. Doodipala Samba Reddy's Laboratory of Neuroscience & Experimental Therapeutics, investigating neurosteroid-based CNS therapeutics across preclinical epilepsy and Gulf War Illness models using behavioral paradigms, histopathology, stereology, and MRI structural analysis.

Concurrently, he contributes to Dr. Jim Song's Laboratory of Microbial Pathogenesis and Immunology, investigating engineered Brucella mutants across tumor cell lines via multiparameter flow cytometry and multiplex immunoassays.

His research interests span neurosteroid pharmacology, blood-brain barrier drug delivery, CNS nanoparticle formulation, spatial transcriptomics, and translational neuropharmacology. He is committed to translating fundamental neuroscience research into clinical therapeutics and next-generation CNS drug delivery platforms.

Current Work

Research Focus

01 Active · 2026–Present

Neurosteroid-Based CNS Therapeutics

Independently designing and executing preclinical epilepsy and Gulf War Illness studies across 25 rodent models, applying 6 validated behavioral paradigms to assess CNS drug efficacy of neurosteroid compounds targeting GABA-A receptors.

Epilepsy GABA-A GWI Behavioral Paradigms
02 Active · 2026–Present

Histopathology & Structural Brain Analysis

Performing histopathological and stereological analysis including NeuN, PV, GFAP, and IBA1 immunostaining alongside MRI structural analysis to characterize neurosteroid drug effects on brain architecture and neuroinflammation.

Immunostaining MRI Stereology Neuroinflammation
03 Active · 2026–Present

Tumor Immunology & Brucella Mutant Studies

Investigating engineered Brucella melitensis mutants across 4 tumor cell lines via multiparameter flow cytometry, cell sorting, and multiplex immunoassays to advance tumor immunomodulation and cytokine signaling research.

Flow Cytometry Immunoassays BSL-2 Cytokines
04 Future · PhD Direction

BBB Drug Delivery Platform

Developing a nanoparticle-based intranasal delivery platform to enhance blood-brain barrier crossing efficiency of neurosteroid compounds — targeting lower doses, fewer side effects, and new therapeutic applications in epilepsy, PTSD, and neuroinflammation.

BBB Nanoparticles Drug Delivery Intranasal
