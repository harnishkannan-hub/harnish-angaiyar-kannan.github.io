About Harnish

Harnish Angaiyar Kannan is a Master of Biotechnology candidate at Texas A&M University (College Station), maintaining a perfect 4.0 GPA. His research focuses on the intersection of neuropharmacology, drug delivery, and translational neuroscience.

He works in the Bhatt Lab investigating neurosteroid mechanisms in GABAergic signaling and in the Ramkumar Lab studying BBB transport and CNS drug delivery strategies. His long-term vision is to develop nanoparticle-based drug delivery platforms targeting the blood–brain barrier for neurological conditions.

Harnish is a published author in the International Journal of Pharmaceutical Research and Applications (IJPRA) and brings training from NSF-funded programs and the AstraZeneca–TAMU Neuroscience Bootcamp.

Active Work

Research Projects

Neuropharmacology

Neurosteroid Mechanisms in GABAergic Signaling

Investigating how endogenous neurosteroids modulate GABA-A receptor function and exploring their therapeutic potential in epilepsy and anxiety-related disorders using in vitro and ex vivo models.

Drug Delivery

Blood–Brain Barrier Transport & CNS Targeting

Characterizing molecular transport mechanisms across the BBB and evaluating nanoparticle-based formulations designed to enhance CNS bioavailability of neurotherapeutic agents.

Neuroinflammation

Neuroinflammatory Pathways in CNS Disease

Exploring the roles of microglia and astrocytes in neuroinflammatory cascades, focusing on cytokine signaling and its relevance to neurodegenerative and neuropsychiatric conditions.

Future Direction

Nanoparticle BBB Delivery Platforms

Designing lipid and polymer-based nanocarriers for targeted delivery of neurosteroids and small-molecule CNS drugs across the blood–brain barrier, with translational applications.
